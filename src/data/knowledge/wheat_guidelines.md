# Wheat Production Technology in Pakistan (Gandum Ki Kasht)
Source: Pakistan Agricultural Research Council (PARC) / Agriculture Department Punjab

## 1. Sowing Time (Waqt-e-Kasht)
- **Optimal Sowing:** 20th October to 20th November.
- **Late Sowing:** After 20th November (Yield decreases by 15-20 kg per acre per day).
- **Advice:** Always try to complete sowing before 20th November to avoid terminal heat stress in March/April.

## 2. Seed Rate (Beej Ki Miqdar)
- **Normal Sowing:** 40 to 50 kg per acre.
- **Late Sowing:** 50 to 60 kg per acre (to compensate for less tillering).
- **Seed Treatment:** Treat seeds with a recommended fungicide (e.g., Thiophanate Methyl) before sowing to prevent seed-borne diseases like Smut (Kangi).

## 3. Approved Varieties (Manzoor Shuda Aqsaam)
- **Irrigated Areas:** Akbar 2019, Urooj 22, Dilkash 20, Faisalabad 2008, Zincol 2016.
- **Rainfed (Barani) Areas:** Barani 2017, Chakwal 50, Pakistan 2013.
- **Rust Resistant:** Always prefer rust-resistant varieties to avoid Yellow Rust (Peeli Kangi).

## 4. Land Preparation (Zameen Ki Tayyari)
- Use a chisel plough or deep plough every 2-3 years to break the hardpan.
- For normal sowing, 2-3 ploughings followed by planking (Suhaga) are sufficient to retain moisture.
- **Modern Method:** Zero Tillage or Drill Sowing (Happy Seeder) is highly recommended, especially after rice harvesting, to save time and moisture.

## 5. Fertilizer Management (Khaad Ka Istamal)
- **Phosphorus (DAP):** 1.5 to 2 bags per acre. MUST be applied at the time of sowing.
- **Nitrogen (Urea):** 2 to 2.5 bags per acre.
  - Apply 1/2 bag at sowing (if needed).
  - Apply 1 bag with the 1st irrigation.
  - Apply 1 bag with the 2nd irrigation.
- **Potash (SOP):** 1 bag per acre at sowing for better grain filling and drought tolerance.

## 6. Irrigation Schedule (Aabpashi)
Wheat generally requires 3 to 4 irrigations depending on rainfall and soil type.
1. **First Irrigation (Crown Root Initiation - CRI):** 20 to 25 days after sowing. (CRITICAL STAGE).
2. **Second Irrigation (Booting Stage):** 80 to 90 days after sowing.
3. **Third Irrigation (Milking/Grain Formation Stage):** 110 to 120 days after sowing.

## 7. Weed Management (Jari Bootiyon Ka Tadaruk)
- Broadleaf weeds (Bathu, Lehli) and Narrow-leaf weeds (Dumbi Sitti, Jangli Jai) cause 30-40% yield loss.
- **Chemical Control:** Spray selective weedicides (e.g., Atlantis, Isoproturon, or 2,4-D for broadleaves) after the first irrigation when the soil is in "Wattar" (workable moisture) condition, usually 30-40 days after sowing.
- Do not spray when it is cloudy or raining.

## 8. Disease & Pest Management (Bemariyan Aur Keeray)
- **Yellow Rust (Peeli Kangi):** Appears as yellow powder on leaves. Spray highly recommended fungicides (e.g., Tilt, Nativo, or Amistar Top) as soon as symptoms appear in February/March.
- **Termites (Deemek):** If termites attack the roots (especially in sandy soils or rainfed areas), apply Chlorpyrifos or Fipronil (liquid) with the irrigation water.
- **Aphids (Sust Tela):** Usually controlled naturally by Ladybird beetles. If infestation is severe (more than 10-15 aphids per spike), use localized sprays of Imidacloprid.

## 9. Harvesting & Storage (Katai Aur Zakheera)
- Harvest when the grain moisture is below 10-12%.
- Store in clean, dry, and fumigated godowns to protect from grain weevils (Sursari). Use Phosphine tablets (Aluminum Phosphide) for fumigation in airtight stores.
