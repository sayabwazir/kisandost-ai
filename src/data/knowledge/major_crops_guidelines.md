# Major Crops Production Technology in Pakistan
Source: Agriculture Department Punjab & PARC

## 1. Cotton (Kapas / Phutti)
- **Sowing Time:** 1st April to 31st May (Depends on the region).
- **Varieties:** IUB-2013, FH-142, MNH-886, BS-15 (Always use Bt Cotton varieties to resist bollworms).
- **Seed Rate:** 6 to 8 kg per acre (Delinted seed).
- **Fertilizer:** 1.5 bag DAP at sowing, 3-4 bags Urea in splits.
- **Pests:** Whitefly (Safed Makhi), Jassid (Sabz Tela), Pink Bollworm (Gulabi Sundi).
- **Pesticides:** For Whitefly use Flonicamid or Diafenthiuron. For Pink Bollworm use Spinetoram.

## 2. Rice (Chawal / Dhaan)
- **Nursery Sowing:** 20th May to 15th June.
- **Transplantation (Paneeri Muntaqili):** 20th June to 20th July (Seedlings should be 25-30 days old).
- **Varieties:** Super Basmati, Basmati 515, Kissan Basmati, PK-1121 (Aromatic varieties).
- **Irrigation:** Keep the field flooded with 1.5 to 2 inches of water for the first 25-30 days to suppress weeds.
- **Weed Control:** Apply pre-emergence herbicides like Butachlor or Machete within 3-4 days of transplantation in standing water.
- **Pests:** Stem Borer (Tanay ki Sundi), Leaf Folder (Patta Lapeet Sundi). Use Cartap Hydrochloride or Monomehypo granules.

## 3. Sugarcane (Ganna)
- **Sowing Time:**
  - September Sowing (Kharif): 1st September to 15th October.
  - February Sowing (Spring/Rabi): 1st February to 15th March.
- **Varieties:** CPF-249, HSF-240, CPF-253.
- **Seed Rate:** 100 to 120 maunds (4000 to 4800 kg) per acre. Use sets having 2-3 buds.
- **Fertilizer:** 2-3 bags DAP at sowing, 3-4 bags Urea in splits.
- **Pests:** Top Borer, Stem Borer. Use biological control (Trichogramma cards) or granular insecticides (Carbofuran/Furadan).

## 4. Maize (Makai)
- **Sowing Time:**
  - Spring (Bahariya): 15th January to 28th February.
  - Autumn (Mausam-e-Khizan): 15th July to 15th August.
- **Varieties:** Use approved hybrid seeds (e.g., Pioneer, Monsanto hybrids) for maximum yield.
- **Seed Rate:** 10 to 12 kg per acre.
- **Pests:** Fall Armyworm (FAW) is the biggest threat. 
- **FAW Control:** Apply Emamectin Benzoate, Spinetoram, or Chlorantraniliprole directly into the whorl (kopal) of the plant.
